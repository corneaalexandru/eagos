# Investigate a weekly review

An illustrative project note for standalone EAGOS. Replace the example with your own context. It supplies no permission to access records or execute work.

## Intent and current state

Determine whether a short weekly review could reduce missed commitments. Success means enough evidence to decide whether a bounded trial is worthwhile. The initiative is proposed; no investigation or trial has happened.

## What we know and assume

- **Observation:** no actual records have been examined for this example.
- **Assumption:** missed commitments arise partly from scattered notes.
- **Hypothesis:** reviewing a small approved sample will reveal actionable commitments that lack a next step.
- **Open Item:** which records may be examined, by whom, and with what privacy limits?
- **Risk:** private information could be copied unnecessarily.

## Next Task

`review_sample`: examine the owner-selected sample, identify unresolved commitments and report counts with source references and limitations. Owner/executor and read scope must be established first. Stop after the agreed sample; do not contact anyone or change commitments.

The output is a short finding in this note. Completion requires checking each reported finding against its source. No Attempt has started. Each later Attempt records its identity, authority checked, result and resource use.

## Decision and continuity

After reviewing the findings, the owner decides whether to trial a weekly review. No Gate is presumed necessary; assess consequence before making a material commitment. If a Process is adopted, record its cadence, owner, limits, missed-run and overlap rules. Documenting it will not install a scheduler.
