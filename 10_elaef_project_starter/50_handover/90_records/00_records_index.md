# Preserved Handover Records

Preserve material completed, rejected, superseded, or expired handovers here when project history requires the full record.

Recommended filename:

`handover_YYYY-MM-DD_sequence.md`

The stable handover ID remains in YAML properties. Use the template at `../../60_templates/06_handover_record.md`.

Do not move an active handover until transfer and acceptance status have been recorded and links can be updated safely.
