# P2 — Controlled / High-Consequence

Use for legal, financial, safety, privacy, regulated, or materially irreversible work.

## P1 plus

- Explicit approvers and delegated-authority evidence
- Access classification and permitted audience
- Data purpose, minimization, retention, deletion, and transmission controls
- Evidence freshness limits and revalidation triggers
- Independent review for material commitments
- Revision-aware writes or stronger transaction control
- Auditable authorization and final-state evidence
- Tested backup, restoration, and incident procedures
- Conditional-gate deadlines and escalation
- Defined closure and retention package
- Exact handover revision or snapshot identity
- Access-transfer and receiver-acceptance evidence
- Formal handover authority and expiry / revalidation schedule

P2 activation should not pass while required controlled fields remain placeholders.
